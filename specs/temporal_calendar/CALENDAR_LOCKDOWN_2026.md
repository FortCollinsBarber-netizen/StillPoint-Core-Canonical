# Calendar Lockdown — 2026 Seed Rhythm

Status: enacted architecture / regression authority

## Governing intervention

The Calendar keeps the familiar named sequence from January 1 through December 30.

It does not rename months, renumber months, reorder months, redistribute ordinary dates, or restore historical month-number meanings. October remains October. December 25 remains December 25. Birthdays and familiar fixed dates retain their names.

The single removed civil date is December 31.

February 29 does not exist.

The annual geometry is therefore:

- 364 named dates
- 52 complete seven-day weeks
- January 1 = Day 001
- December 30 = Day 364
- December 30 -> January 1 directly
- no interannual or leap/intercalary day
- Thursday is the permanent January 1 weekday anchor
- the complete weekday/date grid repeats identically every year

The 2026 weekday arrangement is the seed template. It repeats unchanged through the enacted fifty-year publication.

## Minimal-disruption rule

Disrupt lived reality as little as possible while correcting as much structural irregularity as possible.

Implementation consequences:

- preserve January through December as familiar month identities;
- preserve the date sequence January 1 through December 30;
- do not turn October back into an “eighth month” or perform historical reconstruction;
- do not move a familiar fixed date merely to make the calendar look more ancient;
- adopt familiar 2026 civic/Christian observance positions as fixed Common Calendar overlays where explicitly enacted;
- keep source-specific sacred, lunar, astronomical, and external calendars provenance-separated.

## Clock jurisdiction

The ordinary 24-hour clock remains.

It is the coordination layer for appointments, travel, work, medicine, deadlines, databases, and shared scheduling.

The Common coordination clock uses the enacted fixed standard offset and does not observe daylight saving time.

The named Common Calendar date changes at fixed-standard midnight.

Local sunset, darkness, dawn, sunrise, and daylight are separate creation-facing events. They govern the Sabbath / Lord's Day / StillPoint protected-time layer where specified. Sunset does not rename the civil date. Midnight does not redefine sunset.

Therefore:

- clock coordinate answers when;
- Common Calendar date answers which fixed named date;
- local solar boundary answers what creation-facing transition has occurred;
- protected-time state answers whether Sabbath / Lord's Day / StillPoint is active.

No one layer silently acquires the jurisdiction of another.

## Witness jurisdiction

They may supply evidence and overlays.

They may not silently add a 365th day, create February 29, create December 31, move the permanent weekday/date grid, or rewrite the enacted fifty-year publication.
