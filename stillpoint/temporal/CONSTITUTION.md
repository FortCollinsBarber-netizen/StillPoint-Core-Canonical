# Temporal Authority Layer — Constitutional Provenance (Runtime-Independent)

This document records genealogical principles that informed the architecture.
It is **not** a runtime requirement. Users, models, providers, and institutions
are **not** required to affirm any theology for the Core to function.

## Translated computational constraints

1. A finite receiver does not become the Source.
2. Receipt is not deed; knowledge is not ownership.
3. Standing is not sovereignty; description is not identity.
4. Prediction is not permission; confidence is not jurisdiction.
5. Past truth is not permanent authority over the present.
6. Evidence is not warrant; warrant is not execution.
7. Completion is not erasure; release is not amnesia.
8. Changed evidence may change warrant.
9. Finality must be earned; living subjects may continue producing evidence.
10. No finite observer, model, administrator, founder, institution, classifier,
    or prior state receives infinite title merely because it successfully knew
    something.

## Runtime enforcement

These constraints are realized as:

- separate Claim and Warrant objects;
- claim-to-warrant and prediction firewalls;
- time-bound warrant statuses (ACTIVE / EXPIRED / REVOKED / COMPLETED / SUPERSEDED / REVIEW_REQUIRED);
- continuing evidence ingress that cannot be closed by prior success;
- domain containment (Scope(action) ≤ Scope(warrant));
- re-entry and release-without-erasure primitives;
- durable, non-rewriting historical records.

Robert Emmanuel LaDay remains sole human CEO and final company authority.
That fact does not encode founder infallibility or convert authorship into
epistemic sovereignty over truth.
