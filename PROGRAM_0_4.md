# StillPoint 0.4 — Autonomous Company Operating System

## Program authority

StillPoint 0.4 changes the operating target from an operator-driven runtime plus a
specialized Signal daemon into a persistent autonomous company operating system.

Robert Emmanuel LaDay remains the sole human CEO and final policy authority.
Autonomy is the default inside current standing jurisdiction. CEO interruption is
reserved for boundary crossing, contradiction, expired authority, genuinely new
policy, or irreversible consequences that exceed standing authority.

## Non-negotiable inherited invariants

- Capacity is not permission.
- Permission is not execution.
- Execution is not completion.
- Worker ownership grants no external-action authority.
- Standing authority does not become inherited permanent title.
- One accountable owner; zero to three bounded contributors.
- Historical success does not manufacture continuing jurisdiction.
- Re-entry requires current evidence and current standing.
- Still Point is a review function and is itself reviewable.
- Providers supply compute; they do not own company identity, memory, policy, or authority.

## 0.4 architecture

The primary production process is `stillpointd`.

`stillpointd` owns company continuity:
- fires due durable schedules;
- restores office workers after restart;
- maintains eight persistent office worker loops;
- repairs exhausted retry/task-state contradictions without resurrecting work;
- advances internal work through the existing CompanyRuntime;
- preserves leases, budgets, artifacts, provenance, review, and action gates;
- writes an audit cache for CEO/operator visibility;
- never mints external authority merely because it supervises work.

The eight offices remain:
Orchestra, Author, Press, Signal, Ledger, Research, Builder, Still Point.

Orchestra owns untriggered/recovery work. Triggered work remains role-scoped.
The general Signal office worker explicitly excludes `icloud` and `gmail`
message-received triggers; those remain reserved to specialized mailbox services
until the Signal fleet is absorbed into the company supervisor.

## Earned stage 2 — Persistent Eight-Office Runtime

Stage 2 adds durable office accountability distinct from execution leases. Trigger-created work inherits its office; new queued work enters Orchestra triage; Orchestra persists a plan and explicitly hands specialist work to the planned primary; handoffs are append-only and bounded; failed work cannot auto-resurrect without retry evidence; office health is durable; and `stillpoint enqueue` becomes the asynchronous company front door. This stage grants no new external-action authority.
