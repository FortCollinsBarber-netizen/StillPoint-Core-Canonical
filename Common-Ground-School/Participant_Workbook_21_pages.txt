START HERE | MY LEARNER AGREEMENT
This school welcomes Jewish, Christian, Muslim, atheist, agnostic, and other
participants. You can recognize that a text exists without believing it is revelation;
you can read evidence without agreeing with an interpretation. Your tradition,
identity, and personal history are yours to describe or withhold.

     I will distinguish what I observe, what a text says, and what I think it means.
     I will describe another person’s view accurately before challenging it.
     I will not assign a belief to another person on the basis of their religious or
      cultural label.
     I may decline personal testimony or use a written alternative.
     I may critique the Common Ground School, StillPoint, and their founder by the
      same rules used for every authority.

READING MAP | SOURCES ARE NOT COPIES OF ONE ANOTHER
The Torah is the first five books of the Jewish Bible and also appears within
Christian and Ethiopian biblical collections. That gives us overlapping textual
traditions, not four independent confirmations. The Qur’an is a separate scripture
that recounts many related people and events. Jubilees and 1 Enoch are additional
ancient texts preserved in the Ethiopian Orthodox tradition, historically related to
earlier Jewish traditions.
