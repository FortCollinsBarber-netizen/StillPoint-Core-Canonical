## Calendar law

The Common Calendar contains exactly **364 named dates** and exactly **52 complete seven-day weeks**.

The named-date sequence is:

`January 1 → … → December 30 → January 1`

December 31 does not exist. February 29 does not exist. There are no leap years and no interannual reconciliation days.

Day 001 is Thursday. Day 364 is Wednesday. Because `364 = 52 × 7`, every named Common date returns to the same weekday every Common year. The fifty-year map is one immutable annual grid repeated fifty times, with seven-year, forty-nine-year, and Jubilee position layered over it.

Nothing inside January 1 through December 30 is renamed, reordered, redistributed, or deleted. Month etymology has no implementation authority.

## Clock law

The ordinary 24-hour clock is retained for human coordination. Midnight remains a valid civil coordinate boundary. It does not acquire exclusive authority over natural or sacred boundaries.

Common Clock does not observe daylight saving time. It uses permanent standard time.

Local sunset, darkness, dawn, sunrise, daylight, and sunset remain separately observable creation events. Sabbath and StillPoint boundaries are calculated from the relevant local light events.
