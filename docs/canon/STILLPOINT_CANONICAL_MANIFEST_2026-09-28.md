## Protected Canonical Formulations

- From does not mean separate.
- Belonging without possession.
- Possession frequently begins not with a lie but with a truth that refuses to stop.
- StillPoint does not limit truth. It limits the jurisdiction of a true description over changing reality.
- The wounds remain; the nails do not.
- StillPoint protects continuity from becoming captivity.
- Completion cannot exhaust God.
- StillPoint is the created boundary where finite jurisdiction ends and what it could never own becomes available to relation again.

Final theological consequence: **The future remains God’s, not because the past was unreal, but because nothing created—not power, not memory, not judgment, not institution, not suffering, not a perfectly accurate description, and not even death—acquires God’s right to determine reality forever.**
