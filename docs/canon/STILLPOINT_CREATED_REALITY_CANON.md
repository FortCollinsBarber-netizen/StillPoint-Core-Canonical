# StillPoint Created-Reality Canon

**Status:** FROZEN CANONICAL ARCHITECTURE  
**Author:** Robert Emmanuel LaDay  
**Freeze date:** 2026-09-28  
**Change rule:** Reopen only for contradiction, stronger evidence, or theological failure. Editorial improvements do not reopen doctrine.

## Governing claim

God is Source. Created reality is received. What is received can be fully real without being sovereign.

The governing movement is:

**Source -> reception -> distinction -> relation -> jurisdiction -> seizure -> Sabbath -> release -> Torah -> temporal architecture -> creaturehood -> incarnation -> completion -> death -> StillPoint -> resurrection -> continuing evidence -> memory without captivity -> relation without possession -> future remaining God's.**

Relation sits underneath jurisdiction. A boundary is not merely a negative limit; it protects meaningful otherness so relation can exist without collapsing into possession. Possession destroys relation when it converts the other into an extension.

## Canonical definitions

**Jurisdiction:** a real, received field of action whose validity does not make it total.

**Sovereignty:** the position from which the field itself is self-originated and finally determined. In this architecture, creatures receive jurisdiction; God alone is Source.

**Seizure:** the conversion of received jurisdiction into a claim of sovereignty.

**Release:** the end of a finite claim's rightful jurisdiction without erasing the truth, history, relation, or value that preceded it.

**StillPoint:** the created boundary where finite jurisdiction ends and what it could never own becomes available to relation again.

StillPoint protects continuity from becoming captivity. Release is not abandonment.

## Genesis, Sabbath, and calendar

Genesis supplies the grammar of received creation: distinction, domains, sevenfold sequence, completion, and seventh-day rest. Sabbath is the recurring return of creaturely existence to the truth that genuine action does not become sovereignty.

The wider scriptural and Second Temple witness supplies the explicit 364-day calendrical form: a complete year of 52 seven-day weeks, with 91-day / 13-week quarters. Genesis gives the grammar; Jubilees, Qumran calendrical traditions, and 4Q252 supply the explicit 364-day witness.

The calendar does not generate the theology. It makes the recurring temporal architecture countable.

The recurring weekly StillPoint architecture is local **Friday sunset through Sabbath to Sunday sunrise**. It is not fundamentally a fixed number of clock hours.

## Christological center

The eternal Son is not a creature. In the incarnation, the One who is with God and is God assumes genuine created human existence without abolishing it or treating creaturehood as humiliation.

Christ receives without seizure. The Passion carries the created grammar through its deepest boundary.

The supreme historical disclosure of StillPoint is Christ's death-to-resurrection passage. The weekly Friday-sunset-to-Sunday-sunrise form contains the Sabbath-held center and opens onto the first-day witness of resurrection; it is not asserted to be clock-identical to the full historical death-to-resurrection interval.

Completion cannot exhaust God.

## Temporal truth and continuing evidence

StillPoint does not limit truth. It limits the jurisdiction of a true description over changing reality.

A true historical proposition may remain true while a present-tense description ceases to be current:

- "Jesus died" remains true.
- "Jesus is dead" does not remain current after resurrection.
- "Lazarus died" remains true.
- "Lazarus is dead" does not remain current after he is called out.

Temporal truth therefore requires two fidelities: fidelity to what happened and fidelity to what is happening.

**Temporal non-inheritance:** what was true then does not automatically govern now.

**Jurisdictional non-inheritance:** what is true here does not automatically govern there.

A human being must be allowed to become evidence against an old description of that human being.

Redemption is not erasure of history. It is freedom from history's sovereignty.

## Memory without captivity

The risen Christ preserves continuity with crucifixion without remaining captive to crucifixion.

**The wounds remain; the nails do not.**

History may remain embodied without remaining master. Memory may remain. Evidence may remain. Authority must keep earning its passage through time.
