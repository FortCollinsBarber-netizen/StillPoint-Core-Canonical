## 2. The author selects a March 25 sacred-year anchor

**I select Common Calendar March 25 (Wednesday) as source Month I, Day 1 of the working StillPoint schematic Enochic/Jubilees overlay.** The source year's twelve months use the 30–30–31 pattern four times, for 364 days and exactly 52 uninterrupted seven-day weeks. This source coordinate is separate from January 1 as the opening of the StillPoint Common Calendar civil year; month names and numbers are not treated as interchangeable between the two layers.

March 25 is an explicitly **modern StillPoint design choice**, not an ancient Common Calendar date identified in 1 Enoch, Jubilees, the Dead Sea Scrolls, the Ethiopian Orthodox canon, or the Hebrew Bible. Its fixed Wednesday position permits consistent source-calendar translation. It is not reset to the equinox each year and therefore does not promise permanent seasonal alignment. Natural equinoxes and astronomical seasons remain independently observed.

This working sacred profile has no jurisdiction to establish or alter the sacred calendars of other communities. Religious beliefs, doctrines, Sabbath practices, and festival interpretations retain their own lawful authority. The protected civic interval of Friday civil dusk through Sunday civil dawn also remains separate from sunset-based sacred observance.

## 3. The author selects a 2027 Jubilee epoch and overlapping 49-year rollover

**I select Common Calendar 2027 as Cycle 1, Year 1**, independent of the Common Calendar's 2026 seed and the start of the existing finite publication. Consecutive **49-year blocks** preserve a continuous seven-year and priestly-course count. The fiftieth-year proclamation is recorded as **ordinal Year 50 of the prior sequence and Year 1 of the next 49-year cycle**, not an additional standalone civil year.

The first threshold is **2075: Cycle 1, Year 49**. The first proclamation year is **2076: Cycle 2, Year 1 and the ordinal fiftieth-year proclamation**. Subsequent years calculated under this rule are **2125, 2174, 2223, 2272, 2321**. With 24 weekly priestly courses rotating through 52 weeks per schematic year, the priestly phase returns after six years. The least common multiple of six and 49 is 294 years, giving the selected long-cycle structural recurrence.

The alternate standalone-50 reading remains available under its **explicit, nondefault comparison identifier**, not as silent active authority. Ancient texts provide the 49/50 Jubilee traditions but do not settle every interpretive dispute or uniquely specify this modern project's epoch. Calendar calculations do not themselves authorize the discharge of debts, transfers of property, penalties, rights adjustments, or any other civil legal effect. **Claim, authorization, action and verified completion remain distinct.**

## 5. The nine functional layers are preserved

Maintain all nine functional layers established in the September manuscript:

| Layer | Responsibility and jurisdiction |
|---|---|
| 1. Common Calendar | Coordinates civil dates and weekdays under the current selected version. |
| 2. Civil Clock | Coordinates appointment times; it does not define sacred observance or natural events. |
| 3. Civic StillPoint | Represents protected time; enforceable legal protection requires independent lawful authority. |
| 4. Civic Holidays | Displays public commemoration and closure policies, separately from religious-calendar rules. |
| 5. Sacred / Religious Time | Displays this project's selected profile and independent communities' observances with their provenance. |
| 6. Sun / Season / Local Light | Reports observed or calculated physical conditions without resetting the civil grid. |
| 7. Lunar Layer | Reports the Moon as observational witness without governing civil dates. |
| 8. Long Cycles | Calculates seven-year and Jubilee positions without executing legal release. |
| 9. External Translation | Translates coordinates to other calendars without acquiring their authority. |

The high-level distinction between institutional coordination and astronomical observation is an aid to explanation, not permission to collapse nine operational responsibilities into two central authorities. One layer's ability to translate, describe, observe, or display another is not a license to govern it.

The current civil-year working design remains **364 named dates, 52 full weeks, stable weekdays, and no inserted intercalary days or weeks**. That is a present accountable design decision, not immunity to future revision. Apparent-sunset sacred boundaries and civil-dusk/dawn protection stay distinguishable. The previously corrected permanent Good Friday/Easter seeding must not return.
