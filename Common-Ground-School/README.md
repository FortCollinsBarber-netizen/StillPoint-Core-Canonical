# The Common Ground School

**Six-unit investigative curriculum | Six 75-minute sessions | October 2026**

The Common Ground School investigates observable reality, historical evidence, shared ancient scriptural narratives, interpretation, authority and human consequence. The founding account of StillPoint is explicitly Christ-centered, but acceptance of it is **not** a prerequisite for learning.

## Governing method — Common Ground Ledger

1. **Reality:** What can be observed?
2. **History:** What independent evidence supports or limits the claim?
3. **Scripture:** What does each source actually say?
4. **Agreement and Difference:** What is genuinely shared, and what differs?
5. **Interpretation:** Which meaning is being proposed, and by whom?
6. **Authority:** Who can decide, command, or enforce what, and on what basis?
7. **Human Consequence:** Who benefits, suffers, or becomes vulnerable?

**Agreement first. Differences for accuracy. Interpretations labeled. Authority examined. Learner released.**

## Units

1. Reality, the Day, and the Beginning of Rest
2. The Garden, the Brothers, Human Violence, and Noah
3. Abraham to Joseph
4. Moses, Liberation, the Sea, and the Wilderness
5. Later Figures: Monarchy, Job, Jonah, John, Mary, and Jesus
6. Authority and Human Consequence Across the Whole Arc

All six sessions are designed for **75 minutes each**, totaling **450 teaching minutes**. The materials include **18 Capture-the-Flag source-checking claims**.

## Theological and civic distinction

StillPoint's founder interprets the Sabbath through Jesus Christ and proposes protected human time of exactly one-fifth of a 168-hour week (**33 hours 36 minutes**), including the 24-hour Sabbath **inside**, not in addition to, that duration. This is a **contemporary theological and civic proposal**, not a mathematical proof of its theology or an explicit ancient scriptural command to protect one-fifth of a week. Civil protection in the proposal is not conditional on religious belief.

The fixed-duration proposal and a variable Friday-dusk-to-Sunday-dawn interval are **different timing choices**; the school must not silently treat them as identical.

## Learning and facilitation safeguards

- No learner must profess religious belief, participate in worship, or disclose personal convictions.
- Inherited identity is not proof of what a person personally believes.
- Textual recurrence is **not** independent historical corroboration.
- The facilitator may correct inaccuracies but may not demand agreement with StillPoint, the school, or the founder.
- The school must submit its own claims to the same seven-layer scrutiny it applies to everyone else.
